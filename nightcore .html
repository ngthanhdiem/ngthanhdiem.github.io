Nightcore and sped up questions

What the tool does, and what it doesn't.

Nightcore is a remix style where a song is played faster, usually by about 20 to 30 percent, and the pitch rises with the speed. The result is a brighter, more energetic version of the original. The classic preset here is 1.25×, which means the pitch climbs naturally with the tempo.
No. Decoding, speed changes, and rendering all happen inside this page using the Web Audio API. The song never leaves your device. There is no server, no account, and no tracking of the audio content.
Changing playback speed resamples the audio, so the pitch shifts along with the tempo. That is exactly what makes nightcore sound the way it does: faster and higher. If you want the tempo change without the pitch shift, you would need time-stretching, which this single-file build does not do.
The rendered file is a 16-bit PCM WAV at the source sample rate. It is uncompressed, so there is no additional quality loss from the export. You can convert it to MP3 afterward if you need a smaller file.
BPM detection uses a simple energy-based estimate that works well for steady electronic and pop tracks but can miss on live recordings or tracks with tempo changes. The speed multiplier still works regardless of whether BPM is detected.
